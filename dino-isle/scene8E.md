
# Leave Radio and Head to River

Deciding the radio is of little use without power, you head to the river.  There are a few trees you can see closer to the river but they are sparse.  You don't stray too close to the tress along the river bank lest something is hiding in them.

Up ahead you can hear something stirring.  Creeping around the batch of trees you see a pachycephalosaurus drinking from the water.  You really don't want to test that bony head it has, so you back away and give it a wide go around.  It's more interested in quenching its thirst and doesn't notice you.

Up ahead you can see the fence to the compound. Following the river paid off.  "Thank God!" you mutter under your breath.  Now you have to find some way to get on the other side.
