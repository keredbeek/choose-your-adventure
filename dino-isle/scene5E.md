# Unexpected Intervention!

The two ankylosaurus immediately stop fighting as the enormous brachiosaurus🦕 stomps toward them. With an annoyed roar, it chases them away from the area.

"SHUT UP! I'M TRYING TO SLEEP!" It yelled. The ankylosaurus quickly disappear into the distance, leaving the area peaceful once again.

You stand there, completely stunned by what you just witnessed. Apparently, even dinosaurs have trouble getting a good night's sleep!

With the commotion finally over, you continue making your way through the hills.
