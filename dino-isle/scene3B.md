# Scene 3B: Naturally Curious

You wander through the nearby grassland, keeping an eye out for anything that might help you survive. Suddenly, you hear a series of low, musical calls coming from somewhere nearby. You cautiously peek through the tall grass and spot a Parasaurolophus grazing peacefully. Its long, curved crest rises above its head as it makes another soft call, seemingly communicating with others farther away. Unlike the terrifying predator in the forest, this dinosaur doesn't appear interested in you at all.

You watch quietly, careful not to disturb it, until it wanders farther into the grass. That's when you notice something nearby: an abandoned ATV, its rusted frame nearly hidden beneath the overgrowth.

"Looks like someone was here before me," you whisper, approaching it cautiously. Maybe this old vehicle could be useful after all.
